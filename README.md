Entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 18 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118).

**40 STORIES · 7 REGIONS · ONE PAGE** — a newspaper front page for Wednesday 18 November 2026,
drawn 100% programmatically (Python/PIL, no image generator): masthead, THE DAY IN NUMBERS,
a UTC timeline, 3 labelled hero stories (Xi greets Trump in Shenzhen, Iranian drones over the
Gulf, Voyager 1 one light-day out), 10 illustrated grid cards with CLAIM/CONFIRMED tags,
a dotted world map with numbered pins, a 27-item ALSO list, and a DATE CHECK box naming the
stories deliberately left out (17 November events and not-yet events).

Run: `node main.ts` — copies `entry.png` to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `./output`),
verifying PNG signature, 1024x1024 dimensions, the 1 MiB limit, and byte-identity with the source.
